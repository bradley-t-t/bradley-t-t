## Trenton Taylor

I spent seven years at Smyrna Ready Mix, starting in the yard and finishing as District Manager over 13 concrete plants. Along the way I built Smyrna Tools, the platform the company runs its fleet, people and plant numbers on. Now I run TaylorURL, a web development company in Baytown, Texas.

### Projects

- [Smyrna Tools](https://github.com/TaylorURL/smyrnatools-com) tracks fleet, personnel and plant efficiency for Smyrna Ready Mix across all of its Texas operations. React 19, Supabase and Vercel.
- [kOS Flight Scripts](https://github.com/bradley-t-t/kos-flight-scripts) are my autopilots for Kerbal Space Program. A booster flies back and lands 0.57 m from its target on a barge, a capsule docks with a station, and landers set down on a chosen spot on the Mun and Duna.
- [CryptoFort](https://github.com/bradley-t-t/cryptofort) is an encrypted credential vault with an MCP server, published on npm.
- [taylorurl.com](https://github.com/TaylorURL/taylorurl-com) is the TaylorURL studio site: one prerendered React build that deploys two sites.
